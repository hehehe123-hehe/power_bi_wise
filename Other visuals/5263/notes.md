Open the file called Class Days.pbix from the folder shown above.

Add a column chart with Day of Week on the X-axis and Sum of Attendees on the Y-axis.

Power BI Column chart
We want to investigate the increases and decreases for certain days.

Right click the column for Saturday and choose Analyze > Explain the increase.

Choose to add one of the results to the report as a separate visual

Adding an analysis to a Power BI Desktop report.
Click the + symbol to add your selected explanation to the page.

Now do the same to explain the decrease in attendance from Saturday to Sunday.

Power BI Analyse difference chart
