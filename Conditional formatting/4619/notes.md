Load the two worksheets from the workbook in the above folder, and create a table showing the total number of Oscars won by genre:

Oscars by genre
Here the genres are listed in alphabetical order.

 
Now apply icons to the Oscar Wins column:

Showing icons
This shows one way to apply icons to this column.

  
Apply icons as follows:

Total number of Oscars	Display
Up to 20	A red cross
20 to 40	A yellow exclamation mark
40 to 60	A green tick
More than 60	A green circle
Here's what your final table should look like:

Final table of icons
Your final table should look similar to this (with the icons to the right of the text).
